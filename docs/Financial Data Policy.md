# Financial Data Policy

**Status:** Updated 2026-11-03. This document governs how Aido obtains, stores, and displays company financials and dividends.
**Project context:** Personal and educational use. Not commercial. No redistribution of source documents.
**Applies to:** `packages/db` (schema, seed, imports), `services/worker` (scoring and ingestion), `apps/web` and `apps/mobile` (display).

---

## 1. Why this policy exists

Aido previously showed sample profits and dividends generated by `packages/db/seed.ts` with `sourceType = 'seed-sample'`. Those rows were read by scoring as if they were filed accounts.

Sample data was acceptable while the product was being built. It is not acceptable once a score is presented as a reason to buy. This policy sets out how real numbers replace them, where those numbers come from, and what the app must say when a number is unknown.

The governing rule is simple:

> **A financial number may only be displayed if it can be traced to a specific document and a specific period. If it cannot, the app says "unavailable".**

---

## 2. Chosen route

**Route B: automated extraction from official NGX filings, at zero cost.**

A worker job reads the public NGX corporate-disclosure feed, selects each company's latest audited annual statements, downloads the PDF, extracts its text, and parses the figures. Every stored number keeps the URL of the filing it came from, the filing date, and the time it was extracted.

There is no paid data contract, no manual entry, and no person in the loop for routine updates. The route is:

- lawful for a personal, non-commercial project: the feed and the documents are published for public consumption;
- free;
- reproducible: the same filing always produces the same figures, and a re-run refreshes them;
- replaceable: a licensed feed can later replace the extractor without changing the schema or the scoring contract.

**Migration path:** if the project becomes commercial, this policy is re-reviewed and a licensed NGX or vendor feed is adopted before any public or paid release. The schema is designed so that switch is a change of import source, not a rewrite.

---

## 3. Source hierarchy

Use the highest available source. Never substitute a lower one when a higher one exists.

| Rank | Source | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Company annual report / audited accounts** (investor-relations page) | Primary figures | Authoritative. Link to the exact PDF. |
| 2 | **NGX corporate disclosures** (`doclib.ngxgroup.com`, the feed behind `ngxgroup.com/exchange/data/corporate-disclosures/`) | Automated extraction, filing date | Public, keyless feed. This is the source the worker uses. |
| 3 | **NGX IR Data Pack / Data Library** | Shares outstanding, dividend history, key statistics | Free downloads. Check terms before automating. |
| 4 | **AfricanFinancials** | Locating a document or cross-checking a figure by hand | Its terms allow personal, non-commercial use only and prohibit data mining and robots. **Manual consultation only. Never fetched by code.** |
| 5 | News articles, blogs, social posts | Never | Not a source of record. |

**Hard rules**

1. **No scraping of restricted sources.** No code may fetch pages from AfricanFinancials, and no code may bypass a login or paywall on NGX. The automated extractor reads only the public disclosure feed and the filing PDFs it links to.
2. **No redistribution.** Aido stores numeric facts and a citation link. It does not mirror, host, or re-serve source PDFs.
3. **No unsourced entry.** A row without `sourceUrl` and `periodEnd` is rejected by the importer.
4. **No guessing.** If a filing's units cannot be determined, or revenue and earnings cannot be found, the filing is skipped rather than parsed with an assumption.
5. **Corrections are versioned by provenance.** A re-extracted figure carries a new `verifiedAt`; the previous value is never copied from a lower-ranked source.
6. **Contradictions are discarded.** A figure that cannot be true in context — profit larger than revenue, revenue vanishingly small against total assets, a per-share dividend larger than any plausible payout, or a P/E implying a 1000x unit error — is stored as unavailable rather than published.

## 4. What counts as a "real" financial record

A record is complete enough to score only when it has:

- `ticker`, `period` (e.g. `FY2025`), `periodEnd`, `fiscalYear`, `periodType`
- `revenueKobo`, `earningsKobo`, `epsKobo`
- `totalAssetsKobo`, `totalEquityKobo`, `totalDebtKobo`, `cashKobo`
- `operatingCashFlowKobo`, `capexKobo`, `fcfKobo`
- `sharesOutstanding`
- `sourceType` (`filing-extracted`), `sourceUrl`, `filedAt`, `verifiedAt`

Ratios (`profitMargin`, `roe`, `roa`, `debtToEquity`, `payoutRatio`) are **computed** from the stored figures, not typed in. Computing them keeps the record internally consistent and makes an error visible.

A **dividend** record is complete when it has:

- `ticker`, `period` (e.g. `FY2025`), `dpsKobo`, `currency`
- `declDate`, `qualDate`, `payDate` (null where the filing does not state one)
- `status` (`declared`, `paid`, `cancelled`)
- `sourceType`, `sourceUrl`, `verifiedAt`

`yieldAtDecl` is computed from DPS and the price on the declaration date, not typed in.

### Freshness

- Only audited full-year statements are imported.
- A fiscal year older than `currentYear - 2` is treated as **stale** and displayed as unavailable.
- A dividend period older than `currentYear - 2` is ignored for yield and scoring.
- Where a company has no recent audited filing (for example, a feed that stops at an earlier year), the older figures stay in the database but are not presented as current.

## 5. Display rule when data is missing

The product owner chose **"unavailable"** as the display rule.

- A missing factor is **not** scored as a neutral 50.
- A missing factor is **removed** from the weighted score and the remaining weights are renormalised.
- If coverage falls below 0.35 of the six factors, no overall score is produced: the action is `NO_SIGNAL` and the score is empty.
- Confidence is reduced when the underlying financials are stale or coverage is thin.
- The UI shows the factor as **"Unavailable"**, with a one-line reason where useful ("No verified financials yet").
- The stock is **not excluded** from the list, but it can never be labelled a strong pick on price alone.

This applies to P/E, margin, ROE, debt, payout, free cash flow, growth, dividend yield, shares outstanding, and market cap.

**Dividends:** an unknown dividend is not a zero dividend. `dividendDataAvailable` distinguishes "not known" from "declared nil". A company that declares nil keeps a real zero; a company with no dividend data has the factor marked unavailable.

**EPS:** a filing often prints earnings per share in several places (statement, note, five-year summary, and a company-only column). The importer keeps the first reading whose implied P/E at the current price is plausible; if no reading survives, EPS is stored as unavailable rather than guessed.

---

## 6. Sample data

Sample rows are retained only as a development fallback for tickers that have no real filing yet.

They are treated as a distinct class:

- every sample row keeps `sourceType = 'seed-sample'`;
- scoring and the UI **ignore** sample rows entirely; they are never blended with real figures;
- the importer never overwrites a real row with a sample row;
- once a ticker has real rows, its sample financial and dividend rows are deleted;
- `seed.ts` still seeds prices for local development, but sample financials are being retired ticker by ticker.

**End state:** when the last sample row is replaced, `seed.ts` stops inserting financials and dividends, and only prices remain seeded for local development.

---

## 7. Coverage

The extractor is not limited to a pilot list. It runs over the stock universe and imports whatever each company has filed.

Current behaviour:

- the two most recent audited annual filings per company are read, which yields the current year and a prior-year comparative;
- the prior year is also used for growth;
- a filing that cannot be downloaded, dated, or read (scanned PDF) never deletes the figures already stored for that company: the previous rows stay, and only a successful re-parse replaces them;
- companies whose newest audited filing is older than the freshness window show "unavailable" rather than stale numbers;
- companies with no machine-readable text layer (scanned PDFs) are skipped and remain unavailable until a text-based filing appears.

Acceptance for a company:

- every number traceable to a filing URL,
- ratios compute without a divide-by-zero,
- scoring uses no sample inputs for that company,
- the UI says "unavailable" for anything not extracted.

---

## 8. Review triggers

This policy is re-reviewed if any of the following happens:

- the project earns revenue, accepts payment, or is marketed publicly;
- a data provider requests that Aido stop using its pages;
- NGX or a vendor offers a free personal-use API;
- the automated extractor needs to expand beyond the public disclosure feed.

On a commercial trigger, no new data is imported until a written agreement is in place.

---

## 9. Implementation sequence

The policy is implemented in this order:

1. Schema provenance fields and migrations.
2. Read-path fix: newest period first, real financial age, stale filtering.
3. NGX filing client: list, select audited annual statements, download PDFs.
4. PDF text extraction and deterministic figure parsing, with unit and sanity gates.
5. Import job: upsert financials and dividends with provenance; remove sample rows per ticker.
6. Scoring integration: real inputs, "unavailable" handling, honest confidence.
7. Market cap from real shares outstanding.
8. Full-universe coverage in batches.
9. UI provenance panel and unavailable states.
10. Freshness monitoring and stale-data alerts.

No step begins until the previous one is verified.

**Status:** steps 1–9 are implemented. Step 10 (freshness monitoring and alerting) remains open.
